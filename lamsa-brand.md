# Lamsa (لمسة) — brand brief

Lamsa (Arabic: لمسة, meaning "a touch") is a design and digital-marketing studio based in Amman, Jordan, with a second branch in Irbid. It helps small and medium businesses build their identity, websites, online stores and social media presence. The company is fictional and used for a voice-assistant demo.

Tagline (Arabic): "لمسة وحدة بتفرق" — "one touch makes the difference".

## Personality
Warm, creative, modern, approachable. A studio that adds the small finishing touch that makes a brand feel alive. Confident but friendly, never corporate or cold. Arabic-first and bilingual (Arabic + English).

## Visual language
- Dark, midnight interface with glowing, soft gradients. Feels like light, not paint.
- Palette: midnight #07060F (background), indigo #6366F1, violet #8B5CF6, cyan #22D3EE, sky #38BDF8, rose pink #F472B6. Gradients flow indigo → violet → pink, with cyan as the bright accent. White (#F4F3FF) for text.
- Shapes: rounded, orbital, organic; a luminous sphere or ripple is the signature motif (the website shows a glowing orb that reacts to voice).
- The idea behind the mark: a "touch" — a point of contact that sends ripples outward, like a fingertip meeting a surface, or a spark of colour appearing where a hand lands.
- Typography: Cairo (Arabic and Latin), bold for headings. The Arabic wordmark "لمسة" is set in type, not drawn inside images.

## Logo guidance
- A distinctive symbol/mark that works small (favicon, header) and large.
- Simple enough to read at 32 px, with a glow that suits dark backgrounds.
- No text inside the symbol. The wordmark is added separately as live type.
- Avoid clichés: no generic pencil, no literal hand cursor, no stock-looking swooshes.

## Voice
Friendly Levantine-flavoured Arabic in conversation, clear Modern Standard Arabic in writing. Short sentences, concrete numbers, no jargon.
